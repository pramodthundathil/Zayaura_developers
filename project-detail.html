CLIENT: JOY ABRAHAM • RESIDENTIAL ARCHITECTURE & INTERIOR

Abraham Luxury Villa Sanctuary

Kochi, Kerala • Completed 2026

Client Patron Joy Abraham
Built Area 5,200 Sq. Ft.
Services Provided Architecture, Turnkey Interiors
Design Aesthetic Tropical Luxe Minimal
DESIGN CONCEPT

Sculpting Space with Light, Shadow & Natural Materials

The project was conceived as an oasis of quiet luxury, balancing bold volumetric lines with intimate residential warmth.

Carefully oriented towards cross-ventilation breezes and framed garden views, the residence utilizes floor-to-ceiling double-glazed glass panels, polished concrete cantilever overhangs, and warm natural oak joinery.

Key Project Specifications

  • Materials: Italian Travertine, Solid Teakwood, Textured Micro-Cement, Brushed Antique Bronze.
  • Lighting: Layered cove architectural LED (2700K Warm), custom pendant features, automated scene control.
  • Landscape: Courtyard reflection pool, native tropical greenery, porous stone walkways.
